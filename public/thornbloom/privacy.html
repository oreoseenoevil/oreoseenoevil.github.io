Thornbloom Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

Thornbloom is a mini game on TikTok made by Tarrosa Software Development Services ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the game uses and why.

What we collect

We do not run our own servers for Thornbloom and we do not collect your name, email, contacts, photos or location.

  • Game progress. Your best score and similar settings are saved on your device through TikTok's storage. They stay on your device.
  • TikTok account ID. If the game signs you in through TikTok, TikTok gives us an ID that is unique to this game. We use it only to keep your progress and rewards. It does not reveal your TikTok username or profile.

Ads

Thornbloom shows optional rewarded ads served by TikTok. TikTok may collect data to show and measure these ads, as described in TikTok's Privacy Policy. We only receive whether an ad finished, so we can give you the reward.

Sharing

We do not sell or share your information with anyone.

Children

Thornbloom is meant for people old enough to use TikTok under TikTok's own age rules. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Your choices

You can clear the game's saved data by removing Thornbloom from your TikTok mini games or clearing TikTok's cache. To ask about your data, contact us at the email below.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
